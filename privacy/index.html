Privacy Policy

This policy explains how GuruPaper handles information when teachers use the question-paper builder, sign in, upload lesson material, or use payment features when those features are enabled.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Current beta status: The free beta uses Google Gemini API Free Tier to generate a draft paper after sign-in and consent. It does not take payment or store the original uploaded file in GuruPaper database/private storage. Paid checkout is disabled. Features described below as planned apply only if activated later.

1. Who operates GuruPaper

GuruPaper is an educational tool being developed for teachers, particularly in Sri Lanka. The operator's formal business details and a monitored support email have not yet been published. Those details must be added before the service accepts live payments. This page describes the current design and intended operation; it is not a claim that every planned feature is live.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account and profile details: Google sign-in identifiers and the email, name and profile details made available through sign-in, plus first name, last name and address that you enter in your GuruPaper profile.
  • Paper details: subject, grade, medium, assessment settings, school or institute name, optional teacher name, paper title and other settings you enter.
  • Uploads and results: PDF or image lesson notes you choose to upload, generated questions and answers, and the status and identifier of an order.
  • Payment information: checkout contact details (including a phone number when requested by the payment form), payment status and transaction references if checkout is enabled. Payment-card data should be entered only on the payment provider's secure checkout, not into GuruPaper.
  • Technical and security information: basic request metadata used to protect the service, troubleshoot errors and apply rate limits. Security rate-limit identifiers are designed to use keyed hashes rather than store a plain IP address as the rate-limit key.

Please do not upload student names, student contact details, marks linked to an identifiable student, medical or other sensitive records, or confidential school documents. Remove identifying information from lesson files before uploading them.

3. How we use information

We use information to create and manage your account, operate the paper builder, process the lesson you submit, generate and return a draft question paper, manage an order, verify a payment when enabled, provide support, maintain security, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

4. Lesson files and AI processing

In the current free beta, the uploaded PDF/image (up to 2.5 MB) and relevant paper settings are sent from the GuruPaper server function directly to Google Gemini API Free Tier to generate a draft. Google indicates that content submitted on its free tier may be used to improve Google products, so only upload teaching material you are authorized to share and have checked for student identifiers. Do not upload confidential school records or sensitive/student personal data. Google's own terms and privacy practices also apply to its processing.

In free beta mode, GuruPaper does not intentionally write the original source file to its own persistent database or private storage; the bytes are handled temporarily for the request and sent to Gemini for processing. Google and the hosting provider may process or retain technical data under their respective terms. The generated draft may be saved in this browser for editing, so clearing browser site data may remove it. A future paid workflow may use private Supabase storage and database order records; this policy must be reviewed again before that workflow is switched on.

Free beta generated drafts are kept in the browser editor/local draft storage rather than a cloud order history. The separate planned paid workflow may store generated results and order records for delivery, payment verification and support. A fixed calendar retention period for all account and order records has not yet been published; those periods must be set and documented before any paid workflow is launched.

5. Providers and disclosures

Depending on which features are active, GuruPaper is designed to use Supabase for authentication, database and private file storage; Google for sign-in; Google Gemini or OpenAI for AI processing; Vercel for hosting and server execution; and PayHere for checkout and payment notifications when payments are enabled. These providers receive only the information needed for the relevant feature and may process it in countries outside Sri Lanka under their own terms and safeguards. We do not control a third-party provider's independent privacy practices.

6. Browser storage

The website may use your browser's local or session storage to keep a paper draft or sign-in/session state working. Clearing site data in your browser may remove locally saved drafts or sign you out. Do not rely on a browser-local draft as your only copy of important work.

7. Security

GuruPaper uses request throttling, sign-in checks and security headers. Server-side payment verification and private order access controls apply only to the planned paid workflow when enabled. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a secure device, protect your Google account and do not upload confidential material to an AI provider free tier.

8. Your requests and choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of account or personal information by contacting the service operator once a monitored support contact is published. Some order or payment records may need to be retained for security, dispute resolution or legal/accounting requirements. There is no self-service account deletion control in the current prototype. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and reasonable verification of your identity.

9. Children and student information

GuruPaper is designed for teachers and adult users, not for students to create accounts. Teachers must avoid uploading identifiable student information unless they have a lawful basis and all required permissions; the recommended practice is to remove such information before upload.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, providers or data practices change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top. Material changes should be published before they apply to newly introduced processing.

11. Applicable privacy rules

We intend to handle personal information in line with applicable law, including applicable Sri Lankan data-protection requirements as amended and brought into operation. This policy does not replace legal advice or any rights that cannot lawfully be limited. For information about Sri Lanka's data-protection regulator, visit the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

12. Contact

A public, monitored support email has not yet been configured for this prototype. The operator must publish a working contact method and formal operator details before enabling live payments. Do not rely on this draft as the sole way to deliver a time-critical complaint or payment dispute.
