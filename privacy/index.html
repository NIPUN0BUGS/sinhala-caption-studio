Privacy Policy

This starter privacy notice describes the limited data this website may process. Review and customize it to match your actual services before enabling sign-ups or payments.

Information you provide

If account registration is enabled, your email address, account profile information and authentication data are processed through Supabase to create and manage your account. Do not send card details to this website; PayHere hosts the payment checkout.

Subscription and payment information

When paid subscriptions are enabled, the server may store a user ID, selected plan, payment/order identifiers, payment status, amount, currency, subscription status and current paid-through date. Card credentials are handled by the payment provider, not stored in this website's database.

Local browser storage

The public caption tool can store favourites and theme preference in your browser's local storage. These values are not synced across devices unless a future version explicitly says otherwise.

How information is used

Data is used to operate accounts, verify subscription access, handle payments and cancellations, secure the service, and respond to support requests. Do not enable payments until you add your real business contact and review data-retention obligations.

Third-party services

The site may use Vercel for hosting, Supabase for account/database functionality and PayHere for payments. Review their current privacy policies and configure each service carefully.

Your choices

Before launching to customers, provide a working support contact and a process for account deletion, access requests and payment-related questions, subject to applicable law.
