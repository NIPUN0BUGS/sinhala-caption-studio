Privacy Policy

This policy is a launch draft for GuruPaper. Review it against your actual business, providers and retention settings, and add a valid contact address before collecting real users' data.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, GuruPaper may process lesson notes or images that you upload, paper settings, order identifiers, the contact information needed for checkout, payment status, generated question-paper content and messages you send for support. Do not upload confidential student records, sensitive personal data or material you do not have permission to use.

How information is used

Information is used to create and track paper orders, verify payments, process the uploaded lesson, generate a draft question paper, prevent abuse, troubleshoot failures and provide support. GuruPaper should not ask you to put payment-card details directly into GuruPaper; payment is handled by the selected payment provider.

Service providers

To provide the service, GuruPaper is designed to use Supabase for the order database and private file storage, PayHere for payment checkout and notification, and OpenAI for AI processing. Those providers may process relevant information under their own terms and privacy policies. Configure only the providers you actually use and confirm their current data-handling terms before launch.

Uploaded files and generated papers

Uploaded source files are intended to be stored in a private bucket and are deleted by the generation flow after successful processing when storage cleanup succeeds. Failed orders or cleanup errors can require additional operational cleanup. Order records and generated paper content may remain available to let you retrieve the result and support the transaction. Set and publish a definite retention period before the public launch.

Security and access

Server-only keys must stay in Vercel environment variables and must never be published in browser code. The application is designed to restrict direct database access and protect order status with an access token. No internet service can promise absolute security, so avoid uploading material that you cannot safely share with the configured processors.

Your choices and requests

For a request to access, correct or delete information, or for any privacy question, contact the service operator using the support contact published on the GuruPaper website. Add and verify that contact information before launch. Applicable rights depend on the law that applies to you.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Publish the date and nature of material updates and review the policy whenever a provider, processing purpose or retention practice changes.

This page is a practical draft, not legal advice. Customize it for the actual business and applicable Sri Lankan and other relevant privacy requirements.
