Billing, Plans & Cancellation

The site displays planned Free, Creator Pro and Creator Plus options. Paid subscriptions must remain unavailable until PayHere merchant setup and server-side verification are complete.

Plan prices

Free: Rs. 0. Public caption collections and basic browser tools are available without a subscription.

Creator Pro: Rs. 490 per month. Intended to unlock the server-protected Pro caption collection.

Creator Plus: Rs. 990 per month. Intended to unlock the extended premium collection.

Payment status

These are intended plan prices. Payments are not available merely because plan cards appear on the site. Checkout is enabled only after a PayHere merchant account is approved, Supabase is configured, required environment variables are added, and sandbox tests succeed.

Renewal

Paid plans are intended to renew monthly through PayHere. The exact recurring amount and interval must be displayed before a customer confirms checkout.

Cancellation

Use the member dashboard's cancellation function if the API credentials and cancellation endpoint are enabled. If it is not available, contact the merchant through the real support contact shown at checkout. Explain whether paid access remains until the current period end, and do not promise a refund unless your policy supports it.
